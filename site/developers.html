Status

Testnet rules.

It can be reset

The chain, balances and history can be wiped before mainnet. Nothing here has monetary value.

No uptime SLA

One operator, four validators on one host. It is a testnet, not production.

Rate limits

The API rate-limits mining requests (5 per hour per IP). Do not build on it as a production backend.

Luggom is a reserve anchored by the basket's algorithm: scarce, yet elastic, it grows with the real economy rather than speculation. It needs no USD or USDT in reserve, it is borderless and free (no KYC), and its reference rises 10% per year. Stability anchored in the basket, not in a promise.
