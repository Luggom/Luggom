01

Introduction

Every year you pay tax. That money leaves you and does not come back. Luggom does not give the money back: it turns the payment into money you own.

Luggom is a Cosmos SDK blockchain with a module of its own, x/luggom. The core idea is simple: you pay real tax, you prove that payment with TLSNotary and you mine LUG. The protocol never touches your money and never asks for access to your account.

There are no third parties. There is no KYC. There is no intermediary approving you. What enters the protocol is not your bank balance, it is the cryptographic proof that you paid.

The project is anti-censorship by design and the founder is anonymous (Lugo Vane). Instead of promises, Luggom fixes an immutable reference formula, a supply cap and a reserve in BTC that is not redeemable.

02

How it works

Everything depends on one proof. TLSNotary shows that your payment receipt is genuine without exposing your banking session.

The TLSNotary notary ends the TLS connection outside the browser and reveals only the receipt. Your session, your password and your personal data are never exposed. What comes out is a verifiable document: collecting entity, amount, date and receipt identifier.

01 / pay

Pay the tax

At your bank, as always. Luggom does not touch the money and does not ask for access to your account.

02 / prove

Prove the payment

TLSNotary ends the TLS connection outside the browser and reveals only the receipt. No password, no personal data.

03 / mine

Mine LUG

The notary signs the receipt, the chain verifies the signature and mints. Each receipt mines only once.

The notary extracts and signs the attestation with ECDSA secp256k1. The chain verifies the signature before minting. Without a valid signature, there is no new LUG.

There is no KYC, no entity validating your identity and no third parties in between. The only question the protocol asks is whether the receipt is genuine.

03

The currency

LUG has no fixed price and no peg. It has a single reference: the average of three strong currencies, times three, growing 10% per year.

LUG = ( EUR/USD + GBP/USD + CHF/USD ) / 3 × 3 · +10%/year

The basket is the average of EUR/USD, GBP/USD and CHF/USD. Multiplied by three, it gives the LUG reference. On top of that reference sits a growth of 10% per year, compounded, counted from year zero.

Year 0 $3.60
Year 5 $5.80
Year 10 $9.34

The multiplier and the growth rate are compiled constants. They live in the code, out of governance reach. No vote or emergency can change them.

The reference sets the OTC price and the pace of issuance. It is not a promise of conversion.

Immutable in code

The factor of three and the 10% per year live compiled. Changing them would require changing the binary, not passing a proposal.

No peg

The protocol does not convert LUG into dollars. The basket is a target, not a redemption commitment.

04

Issuance and phases

The cap is 7B LUG per year and shrinks 5% per year. Each LUG mined is split across three phases, and each phase sums to 100%.

The cap is not a stockpile waiting to be mined: it is the maximum that can be minted in a year. Whatever is not mined that year is not carried over to the next — it can never be created again. Example: if only 100M LUG are mined in the first year, the other 6.9B will simply never exist. Real supply is what is mined, not the cap.

The three phases

Phase 1 is the pre-sale. The transition starts when 10M LUG have been mined. Phase 2 begins when the pool and IBC open.

The pre-sale

The pre-sale places 3.5M LUG at $0.10, that is $350k. Vesting is linear over 3 months. Of the proceeds, $100k go to the pool and $100k to security and marketing; the use of the remainder will be decided by the community.

The founder

The founder receives 5% of everything mined, with no accumulation limit. Vesting is a pure cliff of 24 months, implemented in the code: nothing leaves before 24 months and, from then on, the whole accumulated amount is released and flows directly.

The savings reservoirs

The staking and ATOM reservoirs drip 10% per year of balance. The combined yield is capped at 25% per year. Qualification for the ATOM reservoir requires a fixed 100 ATOM.

Fee and dust

The transfer fee is 0.005 LUG. Creating a new wallet costs 0.00000000001 LUG in dust, once per address and at most 5 per hour per IP.

05

The endowment

40% of everything mined, in Phase 2, goes to the OTC vault. It is not a treasury: it is a supply wall.

The LUG in the vault can only be bought with BTC, in minimum lots of 100,000 LUG, at the basket price of the day. The BTC is never sold: it would only move with an 87% governance vote, and only to migrate to equal-or-stronger custody of the same asset — which does not exist today. In practice, the BTC dies there in the endowment.

The vault does not exist to defend the price. It exists to take LUG out of the market permanently and predictably.

Not a treasury

LUG only leaves the vault when it is bought at the basket of the day. Never by vote, never to pay expenses. It is a supply wall, not a box to spend.

Only BTC comes in

The only currency accepted is BTC. The minimum lot is 100,000 LUG and the price is always the basket of the day. The BTC stays put in the vault.

A gold vault, not a debt

The accumulated BTC is an asset, not a reserve to be spent. It is the same thesis as the MicroStrategy BTC treasury — but without the debt and the expenses pressing against it: nothing can force a BTC sale to pay costs, staking or creditors. BTC goes in and stays. It is a gold vault, not a spending account.

06

Governance and immutability

There are three layers, and the order matters. First the code that is not touched, then the parameters that evolve and, only at the end, the emergency.

Real immutability is not a promise of intent. It is what the binary refuses to execute.

Layer 1: immutable code

The basket, the 7B LUG cap, the 0.005 LUG fee, the mining split and the OTC sale rules are compiled constants. Governance does not vote on them.

Layer 2: parameters

The list of authorised banks, the collecting entities and the lists live in the parameters. Governance can change them, for example adding or removing a bank.

Layer 3: Shamir 7-of-12

A 7-of-12 Shamir scheme guards the code. It serves emergencies only: it does not set policy and it does not change the currency.

The quorum and veto thresholds of governance are frozen in the code. Whoever wants to change them cannot do it by vote.

The notaries

Notaries come from a pool of volunteers among the validators, who number at most 180. Notarisation starts with 1 notary at launch, moves to 3 (2-of-3 quorum) and then to 5 (3-of-5 quorum). They are paid from 0.5% of the governance reserve, held in a separate module account. The notary money is not votable.

Mainnet only opens with a minimum of 12 validators.

The list of banks and entities

The tax list has one Brazilian credit union proven end to end, and a whitelist ready to light up more. A new institution lights up when a community member delivers a sanitised real receipt from that institution: we confirm the rail's canonical field, the rail goes live and every customer of that institution can mine. It is a job of evidence, not code. The US and Canada stay off until that canonical field exists per rail. Open Finance, as a second rail, is designed and off, and it is a testnet cohort deliverable.

07

Mining and wallets

Mining lives on the desktop. The wallet lives in your pocket or on your desktop. Each one does what it can do honestly.

The proof requires an environment that a phone cannot guarantee honestly. That is why the miner is a desktop application, written in Tauri. It connects to the node, proves the payment and mines the LUG. Your key never leaves the machine.

The wallet runs on mobile or desktop: balance, receive, send and statement. All signed on the device, with no server in between.

Desktop: the miner

A Tauri application that proves the payment and mines. This is where the TLSNotary proof happens, with network access and a local or remote node.

Mobile or desktop: the wallet

Where LUG lives day to day. Signing on the device and keys that never leave it.

The payment identifier uses only the canonical field, today the PIX E2E in Brazil. It is fail-closed: if the canonical field is not present, mining does not happen.

The GoldHunters

The GoldHunters are the people who teach others to mine. They explain to individuals and companies how to pay IPTU, property tax and other real taxes and receive LUG for it — and charge a commission in LUG for that teaching. The commission is negotiated freely between the GoldHunter and the taxpayer: like an accountant who offers "mine your tax, I teach you, and you pass me a share of what you mine". The protocol neither sets nor charges this commission — it is an agreement between the parties, paid after mining.

For companies

A company in any sector already pays tax — it can mine that payment to receive LUG and distribute part of it to employees. The native pool (AMM) is the exit of that circulation: it is where the market price forms, orbiting the basket above and below. There is no floor forcing a sale below the basket, nor a ceiling holding it — the AMM provides the liquidity for the value to be discovered by the market.

08

Risks and honesty

An honest currency says what it does not do. LUG has no peg, no buyback and no redemption.

There is no peg

The basket is an issuance and OTC price reference, not a promise of conversion. The market price orbits the basket, above and below, without being tethered to it — as it should on an AMM (free market).

There is no buyback

The protocol does not buy LUG. There is no buyback order: the price is whatever the market sets, above or below the basket.

There is no redemption

There is nothing to redeem in the protocol. With no redemption there is no run, and a permanent market discount is possible and survivable.

Residual risk: the single notary

With a single notary, whoever compromises it can mine up to the cap. The mitigation is the distributed notary — 3 (2-of-3) then 5 (3-of-5) —, planned for after mainnet.

Glossary

The terms this page uses, in plain language.

LUG
The Luggom currency. Referenced to the basket and capped at 7B.
x/luggom
The Cosmos SDK module that implements mining, the splits and the currency rules.
TLSNotary
A protocol that ends the TLS connection outside the browser and produces a verifiable proof of the receipt.
Notary
The party that extracts and signs the attestation with ECDSA secp256k1. The chain verifies the signature.
Basket
(EUR/USD + GBP/USD + CHF/USD) / 3 × 3, growing 10% per year from year zero.
Endowment
The OTC vault that receives 40% of mining in Phase 2 and accepts only BTC.
OTC LOCKED
The mechanism for buying LUG with BTC, in lots of 100,000 LUG, at the basket price of the day.
Cliff
A period in which nothing is released. For the founder it is 24 months, after which everything is released.
Dust
A cost of 0.00000000001 LUG per new wallet, once per address.
Shamir 7-of-12
A scheme that requires 7 keys out of 12 to reach the emergency control of the code.
Canonical field
The only accepted payment identifier, today the PIX E2E.
AMM / native pool
The native liquidity pool (x·y=k) where LUG is exchanged. It is the exit of mining: the market price orbits the basket, above and below, with no floor or ceiling.
