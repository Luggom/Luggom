The two applications

One proof, two jobs.

The miner does the heavy work and the wallet keeps the result. You can use the wallet alone — on the phone or the desktop — but mining needs the computer.

Desktop: the miner

A desktop application built on Tauri, written in Rust. It connects to a node, builds the proof request, talks to the TLSNotary notary and mines. The private key never leaves your machine.

Phone or desktop: the wallet

Balance, receive, send and statement. Everything is signed on the device and sent directly to the node, over REST or LCD. It does not go through any server of ours. Mobile mining does not exist: you mine only on the desktop.

Verification

Check before you install.

Each installer will be published with its SHA-256 hash and with the source that compiles it. You compare the hash of the file you downloaded with the published hash and, if you want, you build it yourself from source. If the hashes do not match, do not install.
