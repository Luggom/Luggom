How it works

Three steps, none of them your bank.

01 / pay

Pay the tax

At your bank, as always. Luggom never touches the money and never asks for access to your account.

02 / prove

Prove the payment

TLSNotary terminates the TLS connection outside the browser and reveals only the receipt. No password, no personal data.

03 / mine

Mine LUG

The notary signs the receipt; the chain verifies the signature and mints. Each receipt mines exactly once.

The reserve

Patrimony, not backing.

40% of everything mined goes to the OTC vault. LUG can only be bought with BTC, in lots of 100,000 LUG, at that day's basket price.

The BTC is never sold

It would only move with an 87% governance vote, to migrate to equal-or-stronger custody — which does not exist today. In practice, the BTC dies there. The reserve does not defend the price: it removes the run.

Not a treasury

LUG leaves the vault only by being bought at the basket, never by vote. It is a supply wall, not a box to spend.

Three sources of value

Where the value of LUG comes from.

One center of gravity and two reservoirs. None of them is a promise: all three live in the code.

Cosmos network · ATOM

1. The basket

The reference rises 10% per year, compounded from year zero. It is the center of gravity of LUG: the market price orbits the basket, above and below, without being tethered to it.

2. Staking reservoir

10% per year of balance, funded by the mining split. It prints no new coin and stays inside the 7B cap.

3. ATOM reservoir

A further 10% per year for whoever locks 100 ATOM, up to the combined cap of 25% per year. 100 ATOM, double staking.

Where you mine

Mining is on the desktop.

The proof needs an environment a phone cannot honestly provide. So only the miner is a desktop application — the wallet lives in your pocket or on your computer.

Desktop: the miner

Native app (Tauri). Connects to the node, proves the payment and mines. Your key never leaves the machine.

Mobile or desktop: the wallet

Balance, receive, send and statement. Everything signed on the device, with no server in the middle.

Who teaches mining: the GoldHunters

People who explain to taxpayers and companies how to pay IPTU, property tax and other real taxes and receive LUG — charging a commission in LUG negotiated freely. The protocol neither sets nor charges that commission.

For companies

Mine the tax you already pay. Distribute the value.

Companies in any sector already pay tax. By mining, they turn that payment into LUG — and can distribute part of it to employees. The native pool (AMM) is the exit where the price forms: it orbits the basket, above and below.

No extra cost

It is the tax that was already leaving, turned into an asset. The protocol never touches the money nor asks for account access.

Distribute to your team

Part of the mined LUG can go to employees as a stake — value circulates inside the network.

The AMM is the exit

The native pool gives LUG liquidity. The price orbits the basket, above and below, with no floor or ceiling imposed by the protocol.

Coverage

New banks join by evidence, not by code.

Today one Brazilian credit union is proven end to end. A new institution lights up when a community member delivers a sanitised real receipt: we confirm the rail's canonical field and every customer of that institution can then mine.

Immutability

What never changes lives in the code.

The basket, the cap, the fee and the split are compiled constants: no vote can change them. What should evolve, like the bank list and the tax entities, lives in parameters, with rules. The real immutability is Shamir: it guards the code.

The testnet is live.

The public network runs luggom-test. The binaries are available; the validator guide ships with the testnet package.
